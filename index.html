Care First

Beautiful nails, cared for properly

A great set starts long before the colour goes on. Every treatment at Citi Beauty begins with careful nail prep, and we use hygienic tools and quality, long-wear products throughout.

Our gel colour is designed to last up to 3 weeks — and when you are ready for a change, we offer gentle soak-off and re-apply so your natural nails stay cared for. Bring a photo of a design you love and we will tailor it to your nail shape and length.

  • Careful prep at the start of every treatment
  • Gentle soak-off — never forced removal
  • Designs tailored to your shape and length
